Interactive Concept Map: the Workflow Depth Index

Five dimensions build deep, durable AI adoption in one named workflow; encoding justice runs beneath them all. Hover over, tap or tab to any part of the fishbone to see its 1–5 rubric.

1 · Pick one workflow

Score one named, consequential workflow, such as disease-report triage or case investigation, not a tool or a department.

2 · Score each bone 1–5

Cite observable evidence for every score. When torn between two levels, take the lower. Report the profile, not only the total.

3 · Invest in the weakest bone

Any dimension at 1 or 2 marks the next investment. Date the score and re-score after major model or process changes.
