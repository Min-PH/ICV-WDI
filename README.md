# ICV-WDI: Interactive Concept Viewer for the Workflow Depth Index

An interactive fishbone map of the **Workflow Depth Index (WDI)**, a rubric for scoring how deeply AI is integrated into one named, consequential workflow.

**Live viewer:** https://min-ph.github.io/ICV-WDI/

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7702063

## What it shows

- **Tail (Start here):** the removal test, which asks what would happen if the AI were removed from the workflow.
- **Five bones:** Workflow Depth, Action Integration, User Adoption Level, Structural Reorganization and Outcome Attribution. Each opens its 1–5 rubric with the evidence to look for.
- **Head:** deep, durable AI adoption. Totals of 5–11 are surface, 12–18 transitional and 19–25 deep, and deep also requires that no dimension falls below 3.
- **Base:** encoding justice. For population-serving workflows, results are disaggregated by group, gaps get a documented response, AI-initiated actions are audited, and affected people have a voice.

To explore, hover over, tap or tab to any part of the fishbone. On phones, use the buttons under the figure.
